Features

Everything you need, nothing leaves your phone

Six tools built around one promise: private, offline-first mental health support.

Awareness Hub

Easy-to-read mental health articles that work fully offline. Save what matters, search instantly, listen hands-free.

Help near you

Verified providers across Bulacan with city filters, Near Me distance sorting, and crisis hotlines always one tap away.

Daily check-in

A gentle once-a-day mood check with streaks that encourage showing up for yourself, not chasing numbers.

AI companion

A judgment-free chat companion trained to listen, with crisis keywords that route you to real human help instantly.

Cloud backup

Optional encrypted backup through your own Google account. Your data, your key — not even we can read it.

Gentle reminders

Daily check-in nudges and weekly summaries you fully control. Silence them anytime.

Awareness Hub

Check-in & AI companion

Small daily moments, steady support

One gentle question a day builds a picture of how you're really doing. When you want to talk it out, the AI companion is there — and it knows when to point you to real help.

  • Once-a-day mood check, no editing, no pressure
  • Streaks that reward simply showing up
  • Crisis keywords connect you to hotlines instantly

Your mind is nobody's business. Not even ours.

Every note, mood, and conversation lives in an encrypted database on your phone. There is no server holding your secrets, no analytics profile, no advertiser waiting.

SQLCipher encryption

The entire local database is encrypted at rest with a key derived in the Android Keystore.

PIN lock + Argon2id

Your lock screen credential is hashed with Argon2id. Brute-force attempts are throttled and blocked.

Screenshot protection

Screenshots and screen recording are blocked on every screen of the app.

Backup you control

Optional cloud backup is end-to-end encrypted under keys only your PIN and recovery answers can unlock.
